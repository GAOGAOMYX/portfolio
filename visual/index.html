01 / Typography

Tarzan of the Apes: a 17-page typeset book.

I typeset the first three chapters of Edgar Rice Burroughs’ novel: title page, chapter openers and running text. Each chapter opens with a stacked slab-serif title, and large grey page numbers sit behind the text block.

Typography 2, OCAD University, April 2023. InDesign.

02 / Posters

Beefy Graphic Designer: one structure, three colourways.

A poster series built on a single typographic structure. Heavy diagonal bands cut across a red disc that carries the title, and the body text is set into the space between the bands.

Typography 2: Structures, OCAD University, February 2023.

03 / Information design

Two pieces that put several data sets on one page.

Seafood shows total export value and the top five exporting countries by volume for shrimp, salmon, crab, tuna and cod, with a comparison of growth cycles. Graphic Design 1, November 2022.

The second piece is a line chart of Canada’s median age from 1971 to 2021, built from Statistics Canada data, with short notes at the turning points. Graphic Design 3, September 2023.

04 / Motion

Canada’s greenhouse gas emissions, 1990 to 2021: a two-minute data animation.

I sourced the data, drew the storyboard and style frames, and animated the piece in After Effects. Graphic Design 3, OCAD University, December 2023.

05 / Brand and app

Porcelly: a brand and app concept for a pottery-making service.

Porcelly lets people book and plan a session at a ceramics studio. I designed the name, the identity, the icon set and the app screens.

The concept started from a questionnaire about people’s interest in ceramics, then moved through user profiles, a service framework and user flows to wireframes. November 2024.

06 / Form

Paper structure studies.

Folded paper modules developed into a closed, free-standing form, photographed on black to show how the folds catch the light. Design Process 2, OCAD University, June 2022.
